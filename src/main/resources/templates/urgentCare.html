24/7 Urgent-Care & Emergency Guidance

Life-Threatening Emergency?

If your pet is in critical condition, please proceed to our emergency facility immediately or call our 24/7 Emergency Line at (555) 911-PETS.

Signs Requiring Immediate Emergency Care:
  • Severe difficulty breathing or continuous choking
  • Unconsciousness, fainting, or severe collapse
  • Active, uncontrolled bleeding
  • Suspected poisoning or ingestion of toxic substances
  • Inability to urinate or defecate (especially male cats)
  • Severe trauma (e.g. hit by car, fall from height)
  • Sudden inability to use hind legs or paralysis
  • Seizures lasting more than a few minutes or multiple seizures in 24 hours
Emergency Instructions:
  1. Keep calm and keep your pet warm and quiet.
  2. Do not administer human medication unless instructed by a vet.
  3. Call ahead if possible so our trauma team can prepare for your arrival.
